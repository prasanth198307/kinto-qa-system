What's been built well
The core foundation is strong. Metal rates, karigar master, ghat entries, settlement, chit schemes (setup + maturity + defaulters + redemption), metal ledger, bullion transactions, RFID tags, and metal finance/accounting are all present with proper DB schemas. The jw_ table prefix convention and tenant_id on every table for multi-tenancy isolation is correct. Double-entry metal journals with both debit_gm and debit_inr columns is exactly right for gold accounting.
