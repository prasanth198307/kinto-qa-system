2 role conflicts to fix before going live
Conflict 1 — Godown Incharge: receive AND write off with no approval
Godown has FULL on inventory_grn_scan (can receive goods) and FULL on inventory_stock_adjustments (can write off stock). Combined, they can inflate a GRN and immediately write off the surplus — undetectable without an audit trail and approval gate. Fix: stock adjustments above ₹500 value require Store Manager or Admin approval. All adjustments log user + timestamp + reason code.
Conflict 2 — GRN approval owner undefined
Godown Incharge creates GRN via barcode scan. Purchase Manager has VIEW on inventory_grn_scan. But who approves the GRN to update stock? Currently nobody — the GRN either auto-posts (risky) or sits in draft forever. Fix: Purchase Manager gets an explicit grn_approval permission. GRN status flow: Draft → Submitted (Godown) → Approved (Purchase Mgr) → Stock updated.
