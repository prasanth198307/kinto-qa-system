Must have Important Nice to have
1 — Session & Counter
🏪Open/close session
Counter name, opening cash float with denomination breakup (₹500/200/100…), shift selection (morning/evening/night), manager PIN approval above threshold.
Must have
🖥️Multi-counter support
Prevent duplicate counter names. Show which counters are live. Allow supervisor to view all open counters and their cash balance.
Must have
🔄Shift handover
When re-opening same counter: show last session balance, prompt "New session" vs "Continue". Cashier-to-cashier handover with intermediate count.
Important
2 — Billing & Cart
📦Barcode / SKU scan
USB/Bluetooth scanner support. Manual SKU entry fallback. Loose item lookup by name. Multi-scan same item increments qty. Weight-based items (vegetables, fruits) via scale input.
Must have
🛒Cart management
Add / remove / edit qty. Price override with supervisor PIN. Item void. Hold cart (park bill) and retrieve. Multiple carts per counter (billing queue). Notes per line item.
Must have
🏷️Pricing & MRP control
MRP lock — cannot bill above MRP. Unit price, pack price, bulk price tiers. Auto-pick correct price by qty. Expiry-date-based price override for near-expiry items.
Must have
⚖️Weighing scale integration
Serial/USB scale auto-reads weight for loose items. Price = weight × rate/kg. Label print with weight + price. Tare/zero button.
Must have
3 — Payments
💳Split payment modes
Cash + UPI + Card in one bill. Cash tender with auto change calculation. UPI QR display (Razorpay / PhonePe). EDC card swipe integration. Wallet / store credit.
Must have
🎟️Loyalty & coupons
Scan/enter loyalty card. Points earn on bill. Redeem points as discount. Coupon code validation. Combo offers (buy 2 get 1). Member price vs regular price.
Important
💰Credit sales
Bill on credit to linked customer. Set credit limit per customer. Outstanding balance alert at billing. Credit settlement screen.
Important
4 — Tax & Compliance (India)
🧾GST on bill
Item-level GST slab (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%). Separate CGST + SGST columns. GST-inclusive vs exclusive pricing toggle. HSN code on receipt. GST summary at bill bottom.
Must have
🖨️Receipt printing
Thermal 80mm / 58mm printer. Store logo, GSTIN, address. Item name, qty, rate, amount. Tax breakup. Payment mode. Duplicate copy option. WhatsApp/email digital receipt.
Must have
📋FSSAI label compliance
Packaged goods must show MRP, mfg date, expiry, batch. Loose food items need weight-printed label. FSSAI license number on receipts.
Important
5 — Inventory & Stock
📊Real-time stock deduction
Every bill deducts stock instantly. Out-of-stock alert at scan. Low-stock threshold alert. Negative stock prevention with supervisor override option.
Must have
🔁Returns & refunds
Return against bill number. Item-level partial return. Refund to original payment mode. Stock reversal on return. Supervisor approval for cash refunds above ₹X.
Must have
🗓️Expiry tracking
Batch-wise expiry at item level. Alert for items expiring within 7/15/30 days. Expiry-nearing items auto-flagged for discount or removal.
Important
🛍️Fast-moving item dashboard
Top 20 items by daily sales. Velocity-based auto-reorder suggestion. Dead stock report. Shrinkage tracking (damaged / expired write-offs).
Nice to have
6 — Customer Management
👤Customer profile
Mobile number as primary key. Auto-fetch name + address at billing. Purchase history. GST number for B2B billing. Preferred store location.
Important
📱WhatsApp receipt & offers
Send bill PDF to customer's WhatsApp. Push weekly offers to opted-in customers. Birthday / anniversary discount trigger.
Nice to have
7 — End-of-Day & Reporting
📅EOD cash reconciliation
Expected cash vs physical count. Denomination-wise closing count. Overage / shortage flagged. Z-report print. Cash handover to supervisor with signature.
Must have
📈Sales reports
Hourly / daily / weekly / monthly. Per counter, per cashier. Category-wise sales. Top items. Payment mode split. GST summary for filing.
Must have
🔐Cashier accountability
All voids, discounts, overrides logged with user + timestamp. Price change audit trail. Suspicious pattern alerts (excessive voids by same cashier).
Important
Critical gaps in current screen vs grocery needs:
1. No denomination breakup in Opening Balance — just a single number field
2. No shift selector — mandatory for multi-shift grocery stores
3. No weighing scale integration visible anywhere
4. No expiry / batch tracking in cart flow
5. No UPI QR / split payment mode in checkout
6. No GST slab assignment per item visible in POS flow
7. No "park bill" / hold cart feature shown
