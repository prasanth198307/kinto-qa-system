# Kinto Smart Ops — Industry Fit Guide

## Who Is This For?

Kinto Smart Ops is built for **Indian businesses that buy, transform or move physical goods, and sell them** — with a need for GST compliance, staff management, and operational tracking in one platform.

---

## Manufacturing Companies

### Water & Beverages
Bottled water plants, juice manufacturers, dairy units.
- Production entries for batch-wise filling runs
- Raw material issuance (caps, bottles, labels, concentrate)
- FIFO batch inventory with expiry awareness
- Gatepass before dispatch to distributors
- GST-compliant sales invoices (B2B and B2C)
- WhatsApp checklists for filling line startup and shift readings

### Food Processing
Flour mills, spice grinding, snack and namkeen manufacturing.
- Input raw material vs finished goods output tracking
- Scrap and by-product management
- Purchase orders for agri inputs with batch traceability
- Preventive maintenance for grinders, mixers, packaging machines

### Chemicals & Lubricants
Blending units, paint manufacturers, cleaning chemical producers.
- Formulation-based production entries
- Drum and container inventory tracking
- Hazardous material handling documentation
- Vendor purchase orders with rate history

### Plastics & Packaging
Injection moulding units, flexible packaging converters.
- Machine-wise production tracking
- Preventive maintenance for moulds and injection machines
- Raw material (granules, films) consumption per batch
- WhatsApp checklists for machine startup sequences

### Auto Components & Engineering
Fabrication shops, machined parts suppliers, sheet metal units.
- Machine checklists via WhatsApp for shift startups
- Job-wise production entries
- Raw material issuance against work orders
- Scrap tracking per job

### Textiles & Garments
Weaving units, dyeing houses, stitching units, embroidery shops.
- Fabric inventory tracked in metres and kilograms
- Process-wise production (grey → dyed → finished)
- Job work purchase orders to processing units
- Delivery challans (gatepasses) to brand clients

### Pharma & Nutraceuticals
Small tablet/capsule formulation units, nutraceutical blending.
- Batch-wise production with lot number tracking
- Quality check checklists per batch
- Gatepass-based dispatch for compliance
- GST invoicing with HSN codes for medicines

## Trading & Distribution Companies

### FMCG Distributors
Companies that source from brands and sell to retailers or sub-distributors.
- Purchase orders raised to brand/manufacturer
- Stock managed in FIFO batches with expiry tracking
- GST sales invoices to retailers with credit limit management
- Outstanding payment tracking and payment collection records
- Sales returns and credit notes for damaged goods

### Industrial Goods Traders
Machinery spares, electrical equipment, hardware, tools.
- Vendor purchase orders with quoted rate comparison
- Multi-category stock (mechanical, electrical, hydraulic)
- GST billing with HSN codes
- Vendor debit notes for short supplies or quality issues

### Agri Commodity Traders
Grain traders, fertiliser dealers, seeds and pesticide distributors.
- Seasonal bulk purchase management
- Lot-wise inventory tracking with moisture/grade notes
- Mandi purchase documentation
- GST billing to farmers and retailers

### Pharma Distributors
Distributors supplying to hospitals, clinics, and retail pharmacies.
- Batch-tracked medicine stock with expiry-date-sensitive FIFO
- Controlled dispatch with delivery challans
- Sales returns and credit notes for near-expiry returns
- GST-compliant billing with drug licence documentation

## Service + Manufacturing Hybrids

### Water Treatment / RO Plant Operators
Companies that install and operate water purification plants at client sites.
- HR & Payroll for field technicians and operators
- Preventive maintenance schedules for client machines
- Daily plant reading checklists via WhatsApp
- Expense vouchers for site visit costs
- Client-wise monthly invoicing

### Facilities Management Companies
Housekeeping, security, pest control, and maintenance service providers.
- Full HR suite — attendance, payroll, ESS portal for large field staff
- Purchase orders for consumables and equipment
- Expense management for site-level costs
- Client-wise service invoicing with GST

### Industrial Laundry / Linen Services
Companies that collect, wash, and return linen for hospitals and hotels.
- Processed batches tracked as production (soiled → cleaned)
- Chemicals and consumables managed as raw materials
- Delivery with gatepasses and quantity confirmation
- Client-wise monthly billing

### Printing & Packaging Services
Custom box, label, and carton printers with some stock items.
- Job-wise production for custom orders
- Standard items stocked as finished goods
- GST invoices per delivery with job reference
- Machine maintenance schedules for presses and die-cutters

## Other Good Fits

### Construction Material Suppliers
Cement dealers, steel stockists, sand and aggregate suppliers.
- Bulk inward managed via purchase orders
- Weigh-bridge-based gatepass for outward dispatch
- Vehicle-wise dispatch tracking
- Advance payment collection and credit management for contractors

### Cold Chain / Warehouse Operators
Temperature-controlled storage facilities handling third-party inventory.
- Inward and outward managed with gatepasses
- Lot-wise inventory with date and temperature records
- Client-wise billing for storage and handling charges

### Solar & Electrical Equipment Suppliers
Companies that supply and install solar panels, inverters, and electrical systems.
- Purchase orders for panels, inverters, cables
- Project-wise sales orders linked to installations
- GST invoicing with HSN codes for equipment
- HR & Payroll for installation and service teams

---

## The Key Signal for Fit

A business is a strong candidate for Kinto Smart Ops if it answers **yes** to most of these:

- Do you deal in physical goods — buying, making, or moving them?
- Do you need GST-compliant invoicing and purchase documentation?
- Do you have a team that needs attendance, payroll, or leave tracking?
- Do you operate machines or assets that need scheduled maintenance?
- Do you dispatch goods with challans or delivery notes before billing?
- Do you need visibility into outstanding payments, inventory levels, and production output in one place?
