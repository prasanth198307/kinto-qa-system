1. Payment Management

Overview

Payment Management records all incoming payments from customers. It supports multiple payment modes and automatic FIFO allocation.

Accessing Payment Management

Navigation: Sidebar → Payment Management

Recording a Payment

1 Click "New Payment"
2 Select Customer:
  • Choose from customer dropdown
  • System shows outstanding invoices
3 Enter Payment Details:
  • Amount: Total payment received
  • Payment Date: Date received
  • Payment Mode: Cash/Cheque/NEFT/UPI
  • Reference Number: Transaction/UTR number
4 Allocation Method:
  • Auto (FIFO): System allocates to oldest invoices
  • Manual: Select specific invoices
5 Review Allocation Preview
6 Click "Save Payment"

Payment Modes

ModeRequired Details
CashAmount only
ChequeCheque number, Bank name, Cheque date
NEFT/RTGSUTR number, Bank name
UPITransaction ID
Bank TransferReference number

Payment Status

StatusDescription
ActivePayment recorded and allocated
CancelledPayment reversed/cancelled

2. FIFO Payment Allocation

How FIFO Works

FIFO = First In, First Out

Payments are automatically applied to the oldest outstanding invoices first.

Allocation Example

Customer Outstanding:

InvoiceDateAmountOutstanding
INV-001Jan 05₹25,000₹25,000
INV-002Jan 15₹35,000₹35,000
INV-003Jan 25₹20,000₹20,000
Total₹80,000

Payment Received: ₹50,000

FIFO Allocation:

InvoiceAppliedNew Outstanding
INV-001 (oldest)₹25,000₹0 (Paid)
INV-002₹25,000₹10,000 (Partial)
INV-003₹0₹20,000 (Unchanged)

Viewing FIFO Allocation

  1. Open Payment Management
  2. Click on any payment
  3. View "Allocation Details" section
  4. Shows which invoices received what amount

5. Credit Notes

Overview

Credit notes reduce invoice values for returns, discounts, or corrections. They comply with GST requirements for post-filing adjustments.

Accessing Credit Notes

Navigation: Sidebar → Credit Notes

When to Use Credit Notes

  • Goods returned by customer
  • Price reduction after invoice
  • Quantity discrepancy
  • Quality issues
  • Invoice from previous month (cannot cancel)

Credit Note Types

TypeUse CaseProcess
Simple CreditBasic adjustmentEnter items and amounts
Correct & CreditPrice/quantity correctionSystem calculates difference
Full CreditComplete returnCredits entire invoice

Creating a Credit Note

1 Open original invoice (from Invoice Detail page)
2 Click "Create Credit Note"
3 Select items to credit
4 Enter return quantity for each
5 Enter reason/remarks
6 Review credit amount (including GST reversal)
7 Click "Create"

Credit Note Impact

  • Invoice outstanding reduced
  • GST liability reduced
  • Appears in GST reports (GSTR-1)
  • Linked to original invoice for audit
  • Inventory updated (if return)

Credit Note Fields

FieldRequiredDescription
Credit Note NumberAutoSystem-generated CN number
Credit Note DateYesDate of credit note
Original InvoiceYesInvoice being credited
CustomerAutoFrom original invoice
ItemsYesProducts being credited
QuantityYesReturn/credit quantity
RateAutoFrom original invoice
GSTAutoCalculated on credit amount
ReasonYesReason for credit
RemarksNoAdditional notes

Credit Note Calculation Example

DescriptionOriginal InvoiceCredit Note
Product: XYZ Oil 5L10 units @ ₹5002 units @ ₹500
Taxable Value₹5,000₹1,000
CGST @ 9%₹450₹90
SGST @ 9%₹450₹90
Total₹5,900₹1,180

After credit note: Invoice outstanding = ₹5,900 - ₹1,180 = ₹4,720

Credit Note Scenarios

Scenario 1: Partial Goods Return

Situation: Customer returns 5 bottles of 50 invoiced (quality issue)

  1. Open original invoice
  2. Click "Create Credit Note"
  3. Select the product
  4. Enter return quantity: 5
  5. Reason: "Quality defect - leaking bottles"
  6. Create credit note

Result: ₹X credited, invoice outstanding reduced, inventory +5 units (if restock)

Scenario 2: Price Correction (Previous Month Invoice)

Situation: January invoice had wrong rate, now February (can't cancel)

  1. Open January invoice
  2. Click "Correct & Credit"
  3. Select item with wrong price
  4. Enter correct lower price
  5. System calculates difference
  6. Create credit note for difference

Result: Credit note issued, GST adjusted, outstanding reduced

Scenario 3: Complete Invoice Return

Situation: Customer refuses entire shipment

  1. Open original invoice
  2. Click "Full Credit Note"
  3. Confirm all items selected
  4. Enter reason: "Order rejected - wrong delivery"
  5. Create credit note

Result: Invoice fully credited, outstanding = ₹0, all inventory returned

Scenario 4: Discount After Invoice

Situation: Agreed 5% discount after invoice already issued

  1. Open original invoice
  2. Click "Create Credit Note"
  3. Enter discount amount manually
  4. Reason: "Post-sale discount as per agreement"
  5. Create credit note

Result: Discount credited with proportional GST adjustment

Credit Note Viewing & Printing

  1. Go to Sidebar → Credit Notes
  2. Search by CN number, invoice, or customer
  3. Click credit note to view details
  4. Click "Print" for GST-compliant printout

6. Vendor Debit Notes

Overview

Vendor Debit Notes are claims against vendors for shortages, quality issues, or price differences on purchases.

Accessing Vendor Debit Notes

Navigation: Sidebar → Vendor Debit Notes

When to Use

  • Short receipt of raw materials
  • Quality rejection of received goods
  • Price difference claims
  • Damage in transit

Creating Vendor Debit Note

1 Click "New Debit Note"
2 Select Vendor (Supplier)
3 Reference PO or Invoice (optional)
4 Add Items:
  • Description of claim
  • Quantity
  • Rate
  • GST (if applicable)
5 Enter Reason
6 Click "Create"

Debit Note Fields

GST RateNoApplicable GST %

Settlement Tracking

Track debit note settlements:

StatusDescriptionNext Action
PendingAwaiting vendor responseFollow up with vendor

Fully SettledComplete adjustment receivedNo action needed
DisputedVendor contesting claimResolve dispute

Recording Settlement

1 Open the debit note
2 Click "Record Settlement"
3 Enter settlement details:
  • Settlement date
  • Amount settled
  • Settlement mode (adjustment/credit/cash)
  • Reference (if any)
4 Click "Save"

Debit Note Scenarios

Scenario 1: Short Supply Claim

Situation: PO for 100 drums, received only 95

  1. Go to Vendor Debit Notes
  2. Click "New Debit Note"
  3. Select vendor
  4. Reference: PO-2024-001
  5. Add item: "Short supply - 5 drums"
  6. Quantity: 5, Rate: ₹2,000
  7. Total claim: ₹10,000 + GST
  8. Create and send to vendor

Follow-up: Vendor sends replacement or adjusts next invoice

Scenario 2: Quality Rejection

Situation: 20% of received material fails quality check

  1. Document quality failure with photos
  2. Create debit note for rejected quantity
  3. Attach quality report
  4. Request vendor pickup or replacement
  5. Track until settled

Resolution: Vendor issues credit or replaces material

Scenario 3: Price Difference

Situation: Vendor invoiced ₹550/unit, agreed price was ₹500/unit

  1. Create debit note
  2. Description: "Price difference claim"
  3. Quantity: 100 units
  4. Rate: ₹50 (difference)
  5. Total: ₹5,000 + GST
  6. Reference vendor invoice number

Resolution: Adjust in next payment

Scenario 4: Transit Damage

Situation: 10 cartons damaged during delivery

  1. Document damage at time of receipt
  2. Get transporter/driver acknowledgment
  3. Create debit note with photos attached
  4. Include: Item description, quantity, rate
  5. Submit to vendor for settlement

Resolution: Insurance claim or vendor credit

Debit Note GST Impact

ScenarioGST Treatment
Short supply (goods not received)GST claimed on original purchase reversed
Quality rejection (goods returned)GST reversed on return value
Price differenceGST on difference amount
Damage (goods scrapped)Depends on insurance claim

8. Daily Cash Register

Overview

Track daily business cash flow with opening/closing balances, reconciliation, and variance tracking.

Accessing Cash Register

Navigation: Sidebar → Cash Register

Daily Operations

Opening the Day

  1. System shows previous day's closing as today's opening
  2. Verify opening cash balance
  3. Start recording transactions

Recording Transactions

TypeDescriptionImpact
Cash InCash received from customersIncreases balance
Cash OutCash expenses paidDecreases balance
Bank DepositCash deposited to bankDecreases cash, increases bank
Bank WithdrawalCash withdrawn from bankIncreases cash

Closing the Day

  1. Click "Close Day"
  2. Enter actual cash count
  3. System calculates expected balance
  4. If variance exists, must provide explanation
  5. Confirm closure

Reconciliation

ItemAmount
Opening Balance₹10,000
+ Cash Receipts₹45,000
- Cash Payments₹12,000
- Bank Deposits₹30,000
= Expected Closing₹13,000
Actual Cash Count₹12,950
Variance₹-50
Important: Day cannot be closed with unexplained variance. Enter reason for any difference.

9. Payment Write-Off

Overview

Write off outstanding invoice balances that cannot be recovered. Admin-only functionality.

Accessing Write-Off

Navigation: Sidebar → Admin → Payment Write-Off

When to Write Off

  • Customer is bankrupt/closed
  • Small amounts not worth pursuing
  • Legal settlement for reduced amount
  • Aged receivables beyond recovery

Write-Off Process

1 Click "New Write-Off"
2 Select Customer
3 Select Invoice(s) to write off
4 Enter amount to write off (can be partial)
5 Enter Reason (mandatory)
6 Attach supporting documents (optional)
7 Click "Write Off" (requires confirmation)
Caution: Write-offs are permanent and audited. Ensure proper approval before proceeding.

10. Common Scenarios

Scenario 1: Recording a Multi-Invoice Payment

Situation: Customer sends ₹1,00,000 for multiple invoices

  1. New Payment → Select customer
  2. Enter amount: ₹1,00,000
  3. Mode: NEFT, UTR number
  4. Select "Auto FIFO Allocation"
  5. Preview shows allocation across invoices
  6. Save payment
  7. Oldest invoices marked paid/partial
Scenario 2: Customer Advance for Future Order

Situation: Customer pays ₹50,000 advance before placing order

  1. Go to Customer Advances
  2. Create new advance
  3. Enter ₹50,000 with details
  4. Later, when invoice created for ₹45,000
  5. Apply advance to invoice
  6. Invoice shows "Paid"
  7. Remaining ₹5,000 stays as advance
Scenario 3: Previous Month Invoice Correction

Situation: January invoice needs price correction (now February)

  1. Cannot cancel (GST filed)
  2. Open invoice detail
  3. Click "Correct & Credit"
  4. Enter correct lower price
  5. System calculates difference: ₹2,000
  6. Credit note created for ₹2,000 + GST
  7. Invoice outstanding reduced
Scenario 4: Daily Cash Reconciliation

Situation: End of day cash register closure

  1. Go to Cash Register
  2. Click "Close Day"
  3. Count actual cash: ₹15,450
  4. System expected: ₹15,500
  5. Variance: -₹50
  6. Enter reason: "Rounding differences on small transactions"
  7. Confirm closure
  8. ₹15,450 becomes next day's opening
Scenario 5: Vendor Short Supply Claim

Situation: Ordered 100 drums, received only 98

  1. Go to Vendor Debit Notes
  2. Create new debit note
  3. Select vendor
  4. Reference PO number
  5. Add item: "Short supply - 2 drums"
  6. Enter rate and amount
  7. Create debit note
  8. Send to vendor for settlement
