## Production & Inventory

**Headline:**
Know exactly what went in, what came out, and what was wasted — for every batch, every day.

**Sub-headline:**
From raw material issuance to finished goods output, every step is tracked automatically with zero manual stock adjustments.

**Functional Advantages:**
- **Automatic FIFO batch consumption** — the system picks the oldest stock first, every time, without any configuration or manual selection. Your inventory valuation is always accurate.
- **One-step production entry** — record what was made, and the system simultaneously deducts raw materials from stock and adds finished goods. No separate journal entries, no reconciliation work.
- **Scrap captured at source** — scrap generated during production is recorded as a by-product entry right at the production stage, not as a manual write-off later. Your scrap register is always live.
- **Yield variance alerts** — the system compares your expected output vs actual output per batch. Batches with unusual yield loss are flagged so you can investigate before the loss compounds.
- **Repacking support** — repack finished goods into different SKU sizes or pack configurations without creating a new production entry. Stock adjusts automatically.
- **Full reconciliation report** — see input vs output for every batch in one report. No spreadsheets, no month-end surprises.

## Purchase Orders

**Headline:**
From PO to GRN to vendor payment — managed in one place, with full traceability.

**Sub-headline:**
Stop tracking purchase orders in WhatsApp messages. Every order, receipt, and return is linked end-to-end.

**Functional Advantages:**
- **3-step purchase cycle** — raise a PO, receive items against it as a GRN (which updates your inventory automatically), and close the PO when complete. Every step is linked.
- **Partial receipt tracking** — receive items in multiple shipments against a single PO. The system tracks what's received, what's pending, and what's overdue.
- **Purchase returns in one click** — return items to a vendor with reason and quantity. Stock reduces automatically. No manual stock adjustment or journal entry needed.
- **Vendor debit notes** — raise a debit note against the vendor for short supply, wrong items, or quality rejection. Linked to the original PO for full traceability.
- **Rate history per item per vendor** — see what you paid for the same item from the same vendor in past orders. Negotiate better. Spot price inflation early.
- **Vendor analytics** — understand which vendors you depend on most, which ones delay frequently, and where your purchase spend is concentrated.

## Gatepasses & Dispatch

**Headline:**
No invoice, no gatepass. No gatepass, no goods leave the gate.

**Sub-headline:**
An enforced, tamper-proof dispatch workflow that ensures every truck that leaves has a paper trail — before it leaves.

**Functional Advantages:**
- **Invoice-first dispatch enforcement** — the system does not allow a gatepass to be created without a linked invoice. Material cannot leave your facility undocumented. This is not a setting — it is enforced by design.
- **Dispatch tracking per vehicle** — log the vehicle number, driver name, and transporter for every dispatch. Track expected delivery and actual delivery status.
- **Dispatch masters** — maintain a master list of your regular vehicles, drivers, and transporters. No typing the same names repeatedly for every delivery.
- **Sales returns linked to invoice** — when a customer returns goods, the return is linked to the original invoice and the stock is updated automatically. No separate stock entry needed.
- **Delivery challan on every gatepass** — every gatepass generates a printable delivery challan with all required details for the gate guard and the transporter.

## Sales Invoicing & GST

**Headline:**
GST-compliant invoices that also keep your books — automatically.

**Sub-headline:**
Every invoice you raise posts the correct accounting entries in the background. You bill the customer. The ledger updates itself.

**Functional Advantages:**
- **CGST / SGST / IGST auto-split** — the system detects whether the buyer is in the same state or a different state and applies the correct GST split automatically. No manual selection of tax type.
- **Accounting entries in the background** — every invoice debits the customer's account and credits your sales and GST liability accounts. Your books are always up to date with zero manual entries.
- **Pending payments dashboard** — see all outstanding invoices across all customers in one screen, with the number of days overdue. Know who owes you what without running a report.
- **Customer advance management** — collect advance payments from customers before the invoice is raised. Advances are tracked and automatically adjusted when the final invoice is created.
- **Credit notes linked to invoices** — raise credit notes against specific invoices for returns or rate corrections. Fully traceable. No standalone credit entries.
- **Customisable invoice templates** — add your company logo, bank details, terms, and footer text. Your invoices look exactly how you want them to.
- **Payment write-off** — close small uncollectable balances cleanly without distorting your outstanding report.

## Accounting & Ledger

**Headline:**
A full set of books — maintained automatically, as you operate.

**Sub-headline:**
Every invoice, purchase, expense, salary, and payment posts to your ledger automatically. Your Trial Balance and P&L are live — always.

**Functional Advantages:**
- **Double-entry accounting — automatic** — every transaction in every module (production, dispatch, invoicing, expenses, payroll) generates the correct journal entries. Your accountant reviews, not re-enters.
- **Real-time Trial Balance** — debit and credit balances across all accounts are updated instantly with every transaction. No waiting for month-end.
- **Profit & Loss for any date range** — generate your income vs expense statement for any period in seconds. No exports, no pivots.
- **Balance Sheet on demand** — your assets, liabilities, and equity snapshot is always current.
- **Ledger view per account** — drill into any account and see every transaction that affected it, with dates, reference numbers, and narrations.
- **Outstanding / Aging report** — see debtor and creditor balances bucketed by 0–30, 31–60, 61–90, and 90+ days. Know your collection risk at a glance.
- **Day Book** — every voucher for any selected date, in one view. Useful for daily review and audit.
- **Cash Flow Statement** — operating, investing, and financing cash flows derived from your actual transactions. No manual compilation.
- **Budget vs Actuals** — set monthly budgets per account head and track how actual spending compares. Get alerted when a cost centre overspends.
- **TDS Management** — record TDS deducted on vendor payments. Maintain TDS payable account with full traceability.

## Preventive Maintenance

**Headline:**
Machines that are maintained don't stop production lines.

**Sub-headline:**
Schedule, track, and record every maintenance task for every machine — before something breaks, not after.

**Functional Advantages:**
- **Machine master with full history** — every machine is registered with its type, location, and specs. Its complete maintenance history is always accessible.
- **PM templates per machine type** — define standard task checklists for each type of machine (lubrication, belt check, filter change, alignment). Reuse them across multiple machines of the same type.
- **Scheduled maintenance plans** — assign maintenance frequencies (daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly) to each machine. The system tracks what is due and what is overdue.
- **PM execution records** — when a task is completed, the technician records observations, parts used, and time taken. Creates an auditable maintenance log.
- **Spare parts inventory for maintenance** — maintain a catalog of spare parts and track their stock. When a spare part is used during maintenance, stock reduces automatically.
- **WhatsApp reminders for due maintenance** — technicians and supervisors receive WhatsApp alerts when a PM task is coming due. No one forgets a scheduled service.
- **Machine reports** — see maintenance frequency, downtime history, and overdue tasks per machine in one report.

## WhatsApp Checklists & Machine Startup

**Headline:**
Your operators already have WhatsApp. Now it runs your compliance.

**Sub-headline:**
No new app to install. No training required. Operators confirm machine startup checks on the same WhatsApp they use every day — and supervisors get notified if they don't.

**Functional Advantages:**
- **Zero app installation** — operators receive a WhatsApp message at shift start time and reply to complete their checklist. Their personal phone, the app they already know.
- **Customisable checklists** — design different checklists for different machines, shifts, or processes. Startup checks, cleaning checks, safety checks — all configurable.
- **Automated shift reminders** — messages go out automatically at the configured shift start time. No supervisor has to manually remind anyone.
- **Missed checklist alerts** — if an operator does not respond within the allowed window, the supervisor gets an automatic WhatsApp notification. Issues surface themselves.
- **Complete audit trail** — every response is timestamped and linked to the operator's identity. Admissible for ISO, GMP, and safety audits.
- **Compliance analytics** — see checklist completion rates by machine, shift, operator, and date range. Identify who complies consistently and where gaps exist.

- **HR reports** — headcount, attrition, payroll summary, department-wise cost — all generated from live data.
- **Recruitment pipeline** — track job openings, applicants, interview stages, and offer status. Convert a selected candidate to an employee directly.
